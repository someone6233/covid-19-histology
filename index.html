COVID-19 Module

Learn Histology [some title here]

A patient has come down with a respiratory illness. Your job: put on your lab coat (metaphorically, this is online), examine their cells and tissues under the microscope (again, metaphorically), and figure out exactly how the COVID-19 virus is affecting their lungs.

Along the way you'll learn about COVID-19, look at a healthy lung, prepare a real (figurative) histology slide, examine past tissue under the microscope, and learn during the process. Takes about 15–20 minutes.

Step 1 · Checklist

Welcome, Future Histologist!

Introducer: Thanks for joining me! Our investigation starts with a patient who has a cough, fever, and is having trouble catching their breath. But, first, to explain this, we need to understand three things: what cells are, how cells are organized into tissues, organs, and systems, and how a virus can destroy this organization.

Here's your checklist for today. You'll unlock each item as you go:

  • Meet the virus
  • Review the cell & its parts
  • Learn how tissues, organs & systems fit together
  • Look at a healthy lung
  • Prepare a histology slide
  • Examine infected tissue
  • Compare a normal slide to the patient's slide
  • Watch footage of COVID-19
  • Run lab tests
  • Map the disease pathway
  • Learn about COVID-19
