Your Mission

Something is happening inside a patient's lungs. Your job is to investigate what is happening at the level of cells and tissues.

🕵️

PATIENT CASE #019

Maya, age 13

Maya has a cough, fever, and difficulty breathing. A respiratory infection has been detected.

Your investigation

  1. Explore how a lung is organized from organ → tissue → cell.
  2. Identify important cell components.
  3. Investigate how SARS-CoV-2 interacts with susceptible cells.
  4. Watch a short scientific animation and answer questions.
  5. Prepare a virtual histology slide and choose how to detect the virus.
  6. Examine real microscopy and identify tissue changes.
  7. Connect cellular changes to tissue, organ, and symptoms.
